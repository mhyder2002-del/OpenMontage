Privacy

Campaigns, knowledge nodes, jobs, and discovery items are stored in the operator SQLite volume on the host you control. We do not sell that store.

The console Bearer token lives in sessionStorage on your browser only. Discovery scan uses public YouTube Atom, Reddit JSON, and Google News RSS — no paid tracker SDKs.

Stripe, if enabled later, processes payment details on Stripe’s servers. Do not paste live keys into git.
